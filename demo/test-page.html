A longer selection

Reading in another language is often a series of small interruptions. You understand most of a paragraph, but one expression makes you pause. You copy the sentence, open a different tab, find the language selector, and then try to remember where you were. By the time you return, the point of the paragraph may have slipped away.

A useful translation tool should let you stay with the original passage. The result can sit beside the words you selected, using enough context to make the sentence clear. You should be able to read it, change the target language when necessary, copy a phrase, and close the window without disturbing the page beneath it.

This is also a useful place to check longer outputs. A translation may occupy more space than its source. The popup should stay within the viewport and let its own text scroll. Names such as Odesa, dates such as 3 October 2026, prices such as €49.90, and links such as example.com should remain understandable. Technical terms, quotations, and idioms can reveal different choices made by translation providers.

She said the release was a piece of cake, but the team still had to break the ice with a new customer. We should not count our chickens before they hatch. Those expressions are meant to test meaning in context, rather than simply replacing each word with a word from another language.
