A quieter way
to read.

A small tool should fit into the place where you already work.

Prototype test article English → Ukrainian

The quickest tools disappear into the task. Select a sentence, get its meaning, and continue reading without leaving the page.

A translation window can borrow more than a light or dark theme. The surrounding typeface, paper color, subtle borders, and corner shapes can help it feel like part of the article.

I am feeling blue. This new laptop costs an arm and a leg, so I will wait until the end of the month before buying it.

Good tools give you the answer, then give you your attention back.

Keep the translation close to the sentence. Remember the last chosen language. Make the next interaction just as simple as the first.
